I learned to ski chasing my two older brothers around Big Sky. In high school, I
grabbed a pair of used skis and joined the alpine team, bringing my hockey edges
and drive with me. I qualified for State twice individually and as captain led
the team to State for the first time in our history.

![Buck Hill, MN — slalom racing](../../assets/work/made-for-the-mountains-buck-hill.jpg "Racer carving past a slalom gate, cross-blocking the pole with an outside hand")

I got so hooked on the sport that I apprenticed at Gear West ski shop under a
Paralympian ski tuner until I gathered the skills and gear to open up my own ski
tuning services business.

At Williams College, I joined the ski patrol and ski school at Jiminy Peak,
becoming a certified National Ski Patroller and first responder while teaching
college students to ski and training new instructors.

My wife grew up skiing at Kirkwood, and we have the great joy of spending many
weekends together up in the mountains with our dog Loon.
