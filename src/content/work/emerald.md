I spent five months standing up go-to-market at Emerald Innovations, an MIT lab
spinout of ML engineers building a touchless, non-wearable biosensor. Emerald
licensed the sensor to biopharma companies for clinical trials and used that data
to build its own foundation model for human physiology.

Leadership wanted a second business: selling directly to senior care facilities
as an analytics tool for health monitoring and fall prevention. That meant
repositioning a research instrument for a new buyer, the CIOs and CTOs of those
facilities, who have to trust the data before they'll trust the product. I built
the position around data trust and outcome certainty, and tied the numbers to
what those leaders answer for: rehospitalization rates and star ratings.

I scoped the ICP from scratch and ran outbound to 47 independent living, assisted
living, memory care and skilled nursing facilities in Greater Boston, brought 16
into discovery, and closed with a verbal pilot agreement at a Boston skilled
nursing facility.
