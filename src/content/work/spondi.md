Founded at MIT, Spondi was my primary project, which I built through workshops,
labs, clubs, and hackathons. In 2025, I recruited a team to design and execute an
alpha trial with spondylitis patients.

Bringing on technical advisors from Amazon Alexa and Moderna, we were able to
develop a condensed conversational structure that cut latency and protected
patient information.

As an AS patient myself, I coordinated with physicians, nutritionists,
occupational therapists, physical therapists, coaches, and community leaders
alike to develop a wider understanding of the care network available to patients,
and then created a guide to help individuals find the resources that were
relevant to them. On a personal level, Spondi helped me go from walking only a
few blocks a day to rowing, cycling, and even running again.
